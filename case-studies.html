① The Monday pile · busy specialist

Twenty referrals in. Twenty charts out.

A urologist has a backlog of referrals — each one a separate PDF exported from the patient's record. Instead of opening twenty in a row, they upload the whole set as a single zip.

ChartPrepper processes the batch automatically — every referral becomes its own prepped chart, filed by visit date, with the referral question on top and each line anchored to the source.

On the way: direct EMR integration — referrals arrive without any upload.

② One PA, three clinics · multi-specialty

Same assistant.
Three specialties.

A physician assistant preps charts for three supervising specialists across three offices. Switch supervisor, and the field set, intake questions and note structure follow the specialty automatically.

The same referral gets read differently depending on who's reading it — no re-templating, no separate logins to juggle.

③ The patient fills the gaps · pre-visit intake

Half the chart is built before they arrive.

ChartPrepper reads the referral, works out what's missing for the specialty, and builds a short questionnaire. The patient confirms their year of birth and answers in a few minutes.

Their answers land in the chart as citable sources — anchored to the intake, the same way a referral quote is.
