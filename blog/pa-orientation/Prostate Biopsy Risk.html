    About the models

    PCPTRC 2.0 predicts no cancer, low-grade (Gleason < 7) or high-grade (Gleason ≥ 7) on biopsy. Built on PCPT placebo-arm TRUS biopsies with PSA ≤ 10. Input coding follows the live site: “Do not know” family history and “Not performed” DRE count as No/Normal. Ankerst DP et al. Urology 2014;83(6):1362–7.

    PBCG predicts the same three outcomes from contemporary North American biopsy cohorts. Unknown DRE, family history and prior biopsy each use a reduced model, as on the live site. Ankerst DP et al. Eur Urol 2018;74(2):197–203.

    TUM-RC predicts clinically significant cancer, defined in the source as ISUP > 2, from age, PSA density, PI-RADS and prior negative biopsy. Logistic model fit on 603 biopsies at TUM Klinikum rechts der Isar, Munich, a referral clinic where about 35% had ISUP > 2. Labelled beta in the source, which cites no publication. Not valid after a positive biopsy.
