“When patients ask…” — status & roadmap

A per-topic block of real patient questions with model spoken answers a PA can adapt at the bedside — teaching the framing, not just the facts. This page records what shipped and what we might do next.

Shipped live

Presentations tab — 11 entries

≈200 bespoke questions · grouped by theme · wired into the interactive page

Every Presentations entry now renders a “When patients ask…” block: retention · hematuria · renal_colic · acute_scrotum · luts · chronic_pelvic_pain · microscopic_hematuria · recurrent_uti · stone_prevention · elevated_psa · asymptomatic_bacteriuria.

  • Two buckets: bespoke per-entry answers + a shared canned bucket (Tests & scans, Procedure & anaesthetic, Medication) authored once and merged in, “canned unless overridden by key”.
  • Always answered & grouped: ~15–23 questions per condition in themed groups so a real consultation stays navigable.
  • Teaching “why” notes on the high-stakes framing (validate fear first, never a flat “no” to cancer, don’t over-promise, consent is the surgeon’s job).
  • Files: data in urinary-qa.js (+ qa-drafts/), renderer in qa.js (sibling of patient.js), styles + call site in Urinary Interactive.html. Review page: qa-review.html.

Product polish nice to have

Make the block do more for the student

  • In-page toggles: the mockup’s Show “why” and Quiz me (hide answers, tap to reveal) — currently the site shows “why” notes inline always. Port the toggles into the SPA.
  • Coverage test like patient-coverage.test.mjs: fail the build if an encounter entry lacks an ASK set, or misses its stage’s must-answer questions — so gaps can’t ship silently.
  • Copy button per block (as the patient block has) to paste a clean Q&A.
  • Search index: fold the questions into search-index.json so a student can search “will I lose the testicle”.
  • Consultation-drill mode: a standalone self-test page across conditions.
