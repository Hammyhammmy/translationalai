The history

The history isn’t a form you fill. It’s generated from the referral.

A standard intake form asks the same questions no matter what the referral said — so it re-asks what’s already known and misses what isn’t. ChartPrepper reads this referral, works out what’s missing for the case, and asks only that. Your patient, MOA, or PA answer the gaps, not the boilerplate.

A standard intake form · same form, every patient
1Full name
2Date of birth
3Reason for today’s visit
4Do you have any allergies?
5Please list your past medical history
6Do you smoke?

Doesn’t read the referral. Asks what it always asks — and the patient re-types what the fax already said.

Every answer is a source

Whoever’s there can fill the gaps — the patient before they arrive, your MOA at rooming, your PA between cases. Everything they add lands in the chart as a citable source, traced to who said it.
