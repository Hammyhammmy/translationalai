Refer a patient

Referrals are accepted by fax from physicians and nurse practitioners. Send your usual EMR referral letter; no form is needed.

Who to refer

ProstateElevated or rising PSA for age; abnormal DRE; MRI PI-RADS 3–5; confirmed prostate cancer for a surgical opinion.
KidneySolid renal mass; Bosniak IIF–IV cyst; an incidental renal lesion that needs a plan.
BladderVisible hematuria; persistent microscopic hematuria with risk factors; suspicious imaging or cytology.

Helpful, if you have it

Nothing special is required. These help us book sooner:

  • Reason for referral and relevant history
  • Patient name, date of birth, health card number and telephone
  • Prostate: PSA results with dates, DRE findings, MRI report if available
  • Kidney or bladder: imaging reports; urinalysis and culture for hematuria
  • Recent creatinine or eGFR; anticoagulants

After the referral is received

  1. A urologist reviews it.
  2. If information is missing, we fax your office a list of what is needed.
  3. We contact the patient directly to book.
  4. The consultation note, and the treatment plan where applicable, is faxed to you.

We will publish referral-to-biopsy times once we have a full quarter of data.

Not for emergencies

Urinary retention, heavy bleeding, or fever with a blocked kidney needs the emergency department.
