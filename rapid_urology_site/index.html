Prompt transperineal prostate biopsy for West Toronto

For referring clinicians: suspected prostate cancer assessed promptly, and robotic (da Vinci) prostatectomy when surgery is the right option. Kidney and bladder cancer referrals are also accepted.

Diagnosis

Transperineal prostate biopsy

Under local anaesthetic, through the skin rather than the rectum, a route associated with a lower risk of infection. MRI-targeted where an MRI is available.

Treatment

Robotic radical prostatectomy

da Vinci robotic surgery when surgery is the right option, reviewed alongside active surveillance and radiation, with the same team from biopsy to plan.

Programs

Prostate cancer assessmentElevated or rising PSA, abnormal DRE, or a suspicious MRI. MRI-guided assessment, transperineal biopsy and treatment planning.
Kidney massSolid renal masses and complex cysts: imaging review, biopsy where indicated, surgical planning.
HematuriaVisible hematuria and high-risk microscopic hematuria: cystoscopy and imaging.

Referral process

  1. Fax the referral to 437-523-6877. Your usual EMR referral letter is fine.
  2. A urologist reviews it.
  3. If information is missing, we fax your office a list of what is needed.
  4. We contact the patient directly to book.
  5. The consultation note is faxed to you after the visit.

Treatment

Where cancer is diagnosed, options are reviewed with the patient, including active surveillance, radiation and surgery. Surgical care includes robotic radical prostatectomy.
