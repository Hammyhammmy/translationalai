About us

Rapid Urology is a referral service for urology assessment in Toronto, run with the Division of Urology at St. Joseph's Health Centre, Unity Health Toronto.

Your patient sees one of our consulting urologists, who makes a thorough assessment before deciding on biopsy and treatment planning.

We contact your patient to book, and continue to follow them after diagnosis. We return every call and message, and keep the referring office updated by fax.

All care is covered by OHIP.
